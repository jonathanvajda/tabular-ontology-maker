Create a foaf:Person in your triplestore

Builds a SPARQL INSERT and POSTs it to your endpoint.
If empty, an IRI will be generated with a timestamp.
Advanced settings (endpoint override, auth header, preview)

Endpoint

Your endpoint must accept POST with Content-Type: application/sparql-update. For Apache Jena Fuseki this is often /update.
If your triplestore requires auth, paste the full value that follows Authorization:.
